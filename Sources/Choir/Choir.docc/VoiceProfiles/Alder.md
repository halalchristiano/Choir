# ALDER

`choir.child.male.alder` · conditioning ID `2` · child/male · general

## Character

The quiet, watchful child; old soul; speaks less and means more. Intended uses:
dialogue and narration.

## Supported-envelope target

Pitch −6...+6 st; rate 0.6...2.0×; emotion 0...1; breathiness 0...1;
age shift −1...+1; gender shift −1...+1. Production quality throughout
this envelope is not yet validated.

## Demo passages

1. “Listen. The birds stopped all at once.”
2. “I kept the letter because the handwriting looked afraid.”
3. “Alder waited beside the old wall until moonlight showed the marks no daylight could reveal.”

## Suggested pairing

Reflective child narrator: solemn intent, emotion `0.3`, rate `0.88`. Validate
that restraint remains age-appropriate rather than artificially dark.
