# MARION

`choir.mid.female.marion` · conditioning ID `20` · middle-aged/female · general

## Character

Grace with spine; the judge, abbess, or matriarch in her prime. Intended uses:
narration and dialogue.

## Supported-envelope target

Pitch −6...+6 st; rate 0.6...2.0×; emotion 0...1; breathiness 0...1;
age shift −1...+1; gender shift −1...+1. Production quality throughout
this envelope is not yet validated.

## Demo passages

1. “Kindness does not require us to abandon judgment.”
2. “You will be heard in full, and then a decision will be made.”
3. “Marion closed the ledger gently, though everyone at the table understood that the discussion was over.”

## Suggested pairing

Formal narration: solemn intent, emotion `0.4`, rate `0.9`. Validate authority
without losing warmth at lower intensities.
